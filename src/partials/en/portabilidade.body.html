Mobile number portability

Keep the number you've always had,
with the carrier you wanted.

Number portability only applies to Brazilian mobile numbers: you move your number from another Brazilian carrier to i9c. Bring it over and enjoy 5G where coverage is available, unlimited calls and plans with no contract. It's free, and you won't lose signal. See the step-by-step guide for prepaid and Controle portability (PT). Questions? Talk to us at 0800 800 0900.

How it works

Four steps and your number is on i9c

Step 01
Fill out the form

Enter your details and the number you want to bring to i9c.

Step 02
Send your documents

Attach a photo ID and a recent bill for your current line.

Step 03
We handle the switch

We request the port from your current carrier. You keep using your line as usual.

Step 04
Number active on i9c

Within 3 business days we let you know, and you're already on i9c with the same number.
