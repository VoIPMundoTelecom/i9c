Frequently asked questions

Who is the WhatsApp-only plan for?

For anyone who needs a line just for WhatsApp text messages: a second line, older relatives, kids or devices you only use now and then. It costs R$ 13,99 for 30 days.

How do I top up?

On the top-ups page of our website, with Pix or boleto (Brazilian payment slip).

Does the prepaid plan have a contract?

No. No i9c plan has a contract or a cancellation fee.
